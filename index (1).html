History

There was a young boy named dian, they are poor when he thought of starting a small pastry shop he asked her mother for some money but her mother has nothing to give, so instead of giving up he found a part time job cleaning restaurant after 6 months of hard work he finally saved enough money to start a small pastry shop so he asked his mother for donut recipes his mother was a former baker but later on got bankrupt because of the war that happend she was so moved that her son wants to be baker like her that she taught her the secret family recipe to him that lead to the creation of Donut Eats, it started small only him and his mother but as time passed on the locals loved the pastry shop and after many hard grueling years it started to branch out to other locations and is now the most famous pastry shop in the Philippines and it dreams of having other locations in the whole world DONUT EATS remember that name
